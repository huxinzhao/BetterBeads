Place PNG or JPG/JPEG images in this folder, then choose My designs > Import image > Browse imports.
Limits: 20 MB, 4096 pixels per side, 16 million pixels total.
Original images are not stored in your save. Keep your own backup before updating the mod.

将 PNG、JPG 或 JPEG 图片放在这个文件夹，即可在「我的图纸 → 导入参考图」中选择。
图片不会自动导入或上传。单个文件最大 20 MB，单边最大 4096 像素。
